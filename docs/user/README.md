## The short version

The app has three parts and they build on each other:

1. **Profiles** describe your rifle and your load
2. **The calculator** predicts a firing solution from those plus the conditions
3. **The log** records what you actually dialled and where the round actually went

The third is the one that matters. A solver works from published figures and an
assumed atmosphere; your logged DOPE contains your barrel's real velocity, your
scope's real tracking and your load's real behaviour. Past a few hundred yards
the log wins, and the app is built around that.
